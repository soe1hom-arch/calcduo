Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Overview

CalcDuo ("the app") is a calculator application for Android developed by Wandi (soe1hom-arch). This policy explains that the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

The app collects no personal data:

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs are used.
  • No internet access is required — the app works fully offline.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or other device data is accessed.

Permissions

The app requests one permission:

  • Vibrate — used only for haptic feedback when keys are pressed. This is handled entirely on your device.

Local Storage

Your calculator settings and notes are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere. They stay on your phone even when you uninstall or clear app data.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party services, trackers, or analytics providers.

Children's Privacy

The app is safe for all ages and does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on the CalcDuo GitHub repository.
