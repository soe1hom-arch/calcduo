✨ Features

Built for Productivity

Every feature designed to make multi-calculation effortless. From students to professionals.

🔄

Dual Calculator

Two independent calculators side-by-side. Compare results, work with multiple numbers simultaneously.

📑

Tab System

Create, rename, and switch between calculator tabs. Organize your calculations by topic or project.

🔢

Advanced Math

Parentheses, power (xʸ), square root, π, e, percentage, sign toggle — everything you need.

💾

Memory Functions

MC, MR, M+, M− with a visual memory indicator. Never lose track of stored values.

📋

Calculation History

Browse and review your recent calculations. Tap any entry to reuse it.

📝

Notes Panel

Built-in notepad alongside your work. Jot down numbers, ideas, or results without switching apps.

🌓

Light & Dark Theme

Auto-switches with system settings. Beautiful in both modes with thoughtful color design.

📳

Haptic Feedback

Tactile response on every key press. Feels like a premium physical calculator.

📡

Fully Offline

No internet required. Your data stays on your device. Private, fast, and always available.
